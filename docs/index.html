UPDATE TACT

Install the latest version on your TACT with a USB cable.

Before you start
  1. Use a computer (Windows, Mac or Chromebook) with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Switch your TACT on and wait for the main menu.
  3. Connect it to the computer with a USB cable that can carry data.

This browser can't update TACT.

Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. Phones and tablets can't do it.

This page must be opened over https.
Latest version: 1.0.4
Then
  1. Click UPDATE TACT.
  2. In the box that appears, click your TACT in the list, then Connect.
  3. Click Install, then Install again to confirm.
  4. Wait until it says the installation is complete (about a minute). Don't unplug it while it updates.
  5. Click Close, unplug the cable. Your TACT restarts with the new version.

Your settings, saved Wi-Fi networks and stats are kept.

Step-by-step help, and what to do if something goes wrong: Updating by USB. Every version: GitHub Releases.
