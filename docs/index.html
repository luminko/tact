UPDATE TACT

Install the latest version on your TACT with a USB cable.

Before you start
  1. Use a computer (Windows, Mac or Chromebook) with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Switch your TACT on and wait for the main menu.
  3. Connect it to the computer with a USB cable that can carry data.

This browser can't update TACT.

Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. Phones and tablets can't do it.

Connecting to TACT...

Don't unplug TACT or close this page.

What happens
  1. Click UPDATE TACT.
  2. A small box opens: click your TACT in the list, then Connect.
  3. Wait while it updates (about a minute).
  4. When it says Update complete, unplug the cable. Your TACT restarts with the new version.

Your settings, saved Wi-Fi networks and stats are kept.
